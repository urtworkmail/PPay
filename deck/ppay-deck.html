The problem

Getting paid in Pakistan takes four integrations
and still leaves gaps

Fragmented rails
Cards go through one gateway, JazzCash and Easypaisa through another, bank transfer through a third. Each has its own API, its own dashboard, its own reconciliation.
Months, not hours
Legacy bank gateways are quoted in months of integration work. International platforms integrate faster but do not support the wallets most Pakistani customers actually use.
Fraud is an afterthought
Risk tooling is usually something you bolt on after a chargeback, not something that runs before the charge is attempted.

The result is that a business either accepts fewer payment methods than its customers want, or maintains four integrations and reconciles them by hand.

The solution

One integration. Every method.
Risk checked before the rail.

  • One API, one dashboard. Card, wallet, and bank transfer on a single hosted checkout, or against the REST API if you want your own flow.
  • Sentinel runs inside the payment path. Six deterministic signals score every attempt. A score of 75 or higher blocks it before any rail is called.
  • PKR as a first class currency. Amounts in paisas, settlement in PKR, no FX layer to reason about.
  • Built for the people who live in the dashboard. Signed webhooks, an immutable event log, an audit trail on every action, role based access.

Differentiator · positioning

We say exactly what is built,
and exactly what is not

PPay runs in sandbox. It does not hold a PSO or EMI licence and does not custody funds, so no real money moves yet. Rather than hide that, we made it a product principle: every page in the app and on the marketing site labels its own status.

Live in sandbox
Twelve products, an immutable event log, signed webhooks with retries, settlement batching, and the full merchant dashboard.
Being worked on
The licensed banking partnership that will custody funds, the go live review flow, and a mobile dashboard.
Not started
Real money movement, Sentinel custom rules, device fingerprinting, split payments, and card present terminals.

A payments company that overstates what it does is a payments company you cannot trust with money. The discipline is the pitch.

How it is built

Cross mode data leakage is
a structurally impossible bug

  • Schema separated by mode. Sandbox and production data live in separate Postgres schemas, swapped per request. There is no shared table for a missed filter to forget.
  • Idempotency is required, not optional. Checkout session creation demands an Idempotency-Key, so a retried request can never double charge.
  • No delete policy. Records are archived, never destroyed, with an audit log entry on every account affecting action.
  • Events are immutable. An event is written whether or not any webhook endpoint was listening, so history never depends on configuration.

What comes next

The path from sandbox to real money

Now
Licensed partner arrangement
Structuring the partnership with a bank or PSP that will hold funds. PPay stays the software layer and never custodies money itself.

Go live review flow
The KYC and account review that gates a merchant moving from sandbox to live. There is deliberately no code path that auto approves real money processing.

A no code rule builder with per account thresholds. Today's threshold is fixed and today's signals use IP address only.
