Why we're building this

Integrating payments should take hours, not months

Pakistani businesses have had to choose between legacy bank gateways that take months to integrate, or international platforms that don't support local mobile wallets and aren't calibrated for local fraud patterns.

We think a modern gateway has to support JazzCash, Easypaisa, and Raast natively, alongside Visa and Mastercard — and that a genuinely good developer experience shouldn't be restricted to Silicon Valley.

How we build
Developer first
Clean REST APIs, signed webhooks with retries, and documentation that matches the actual behaviour.
Radical honesty
No fake stats and no phantom features. Every product page says what's live and what's still on the roadmap.
Security by default
Sentinel runs inside the payment pipeline, before authorization — not as an add-on you remember to switch on.
