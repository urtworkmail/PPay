For ecommerce & retail

Don't lose a sale because you couldn't take their money

A customer who wants to pay by wallet and only sees a card form doesn't come back later — they just buy from whoever accepted the payment method they had open. That's the gap this closes.

  • Card, wallet, and bank transfer on one hosted page — the customer picks what they already have, you build the integration once.
  • Sentinel scores the attempt before authorization runs, so a card-testing run shows up as a block, not a stack of refunds and chargebacks to clean up afterward.
  • Settlement batches you can actually read — gross, fee, and net broken out per batch, not one net number you have to trust.

For SaaS & recurring revenue

Stop losing customers to a card that quietly expired

Most subscription churn isn't a customer deciding to leave — it's a renewal that failed silently and nobody followed up. A retry schedule catches almost all of it before it becomes a support ticket.

  • A failed renewal retries automatically on a fixed schedule — three attempts — before the subscription is marked unpaid.
  • The first invoice force-saves a payment method, so there's no separate "add a card" step a new customer can abandon halfway through.
  • Every cycle produces a real, numbered invoice — the same object a one-off customer gets, so your books never need two different code paths for the same kind of charge.

Get paid without building anything

Most selling in Pakistan starts in a chat, not a checkout funnel — a WhatsApp thread, a DM, an Instagram comment. A payment link turns that conversation into a paid order without asking the buyer to leave it.

  • A link is live the moment you create a product and a price — no website, no code, no approval wait.

For marketplaces, agencies & multi-seller platforms

Know exactly whose money is whose

The moment more than one person's money moves through the same account, "what happened to this payment" stops being a question you can answer from memory — it needs to be a record you can point to.

  • Every settlement batch is itemised down to the individual transaction, so reconciling which seller a payment belonged to isn't a spreadsheet exercise.
  • Role-based access means a support agent can look up a dispute without being able to touch payout settings.
  • An audit log entry on every account-affecting action, so "who changed this, and when" is a fact you can look up, not a guess.
