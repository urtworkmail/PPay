How it works

The actual sequence, not a sales pitch

Sign up, pick a way to collect a payment, and the rest updates itself.

1
Sign up
Create an account — a sandbox API key is generated automatically, no approval wait.
2
Pick a way to collect payment
A payment link, an invoice, hosted Checkout, or your own integration against the API.
3
Sentinel scores it, then the customer pays
Test cards/wallets produce deterministic outcomes — one always declines, one always succeeds.
4
Dashboard + notifications update instantly
No manual refresh — the transaction list, volume chart, and your activity feed update the moment it completes.
5
Nightly settlement
A scheduled job batches the day's payments — see it on Balances & Payouts.
6
Refund, or respond to a dispute
Any unsettled payment can be refunded; a chargeback goes to Disputes with a 7-day response clock.
