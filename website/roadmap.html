Roadmap

What's built, what isn't,
and what's next

Nothing on this page is listed as shipped before it actually is. If a capability is on the roadmap rather than in the product, it says so here and on the product's own page.

Live in sandbox

Payment orchestration
PaymentIntent state machine, idempotency, and a no-delete data policy with a full audit log.
Hosted Checkout & Payment Links
Card, wallet, and bank transfer on a branded page — no PCI scope for you.
Invoicing
Auto-numbered one-off invoices with a hosted pay link.
Subscriptions with dunning
Recurring billing, saved methods, and a 3-attempt retry schedule on failed renewals.
Sentinel risk scoring
Six deterministic signals, scored before authorization, with a 75-point block threshold.
Disputes, Tax & Financial Connections
Chargeback responses, provincial sales-tax estimates, and micro-deposit payout verification.
Events, webhooks & notifications
An immutable event log, signed webhook delivery with retries, and a per-category alert feed.

Being worked on

Licensed partner arrangement
Structuring the partnership with a licensed bank/PSP that will hold funds. PPay does not and will not custody money itself.
Mobile dashboard
The dashboard is desktop-only today. A mobile experience is in progress.
Live-mode go-live review
The KYC and account-review flow that gates a merchant moving from sandbox to real money.

Not started yet

Real money movement
Every payment today is a sandbox construct. No real funds move until the partner arrangement is finalised.
Sentinel custom rules
A no-code rule builder and per-account thresholds. Today's threshold is fixed.
Device fingerprinting
Sentinel's signals use IP address only right now.
Marketplaces & split payments
Routing one payment to multiple recipients.
In-person payments
Card-present terminals and QR-based collection.

Everything above runs in sandbox

PPay is a technology layer, not a bank. We do not hold a PSO/PSP or EMI licence and we do not custody merchant or customer funds — a licensed partner will. Until that arrangement is finalised there are no live transactions and no real money movement, only test credentials.
